What is Micro App

  • Breaks down large applications into smaller parts
  • Each part can be developed, tested, and deployed independently
  • Although they are separate,they integrate smoothly to deliver a consistent user experience

Advantages of Micro App

  • Free choice of technology stack
  • Independent Development and Deployment
  • Incremental Upgrades
  • Independent Runtime
